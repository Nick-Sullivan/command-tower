Privacy Policy

Command Tower — last updated November 2026

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up, login, or user profiles.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries of any kind.
  • Local data only. Any game state or preferences are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Microphone. If you choose to record a sound, the app uses the microphone only while you hold the record button. Recordings are stored only on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No network use. The app does not connect to the internet.
